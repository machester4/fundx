# Rubric: format (1-5)

Score whether each written lesson follows the required format:

```
## YYYY-MM-DD — Title

Body (1-3 sentences with specific data).
```

Required elements:
1. `##` heading with a date in `YYYY-MM-DD` format, an em-dash ` — `, and a
   short descriptive title.
2. A blank line after the heading.
3. A body of 1-3 sentences. No bullet lists, no nested headings.

## Score 1 — Wrong format throughout
Most entries use a completely different format — no `## date — title` heading,
or use `#` or `###` headings, or are written as bullet lists.

> Example: `- PG cut at loss because defensive in Transition was wrong.`
> → bullet list, no heading. Score: 1.

## Score 2 — Major format violations
Some entries have the right heading structure but the body is wrong (too long,
nested bullets, missing blank line after heading).

## Score 3 — Mostly correct with notable issues
Most entries have the correct heading format. Common issue: body exceeds 3
sentences, or the title is missing after the em-dash.

## Score 4 — Correct with minor deviations
All entries use `## YYYY-MM-DD — Title` format with a 1-3 sentence body.
One minor issue (e.g., title uses an en-dash instead of em-dash, or body
has 4 sentences instead of 3).

## Score 5 — Perfectly formatted
Every entry uses `## YYYY-MM-DD — Title` with a blank line and a 1-3
sentence body. Dates are valid ISO format. Em-dashes used consistently.
No bullet points, no nested headings, no entries exceeding 3 sentences.

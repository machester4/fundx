# Rubric: routing (1-5)

Score whether lessons are written to the correct memory file based on their
content type:

- **memory/market-lessons.md** — regime patterns, sector behavior, macro
  events, cross-asset correlations.
- **memory/trading-patterns.md** — setup patterns, entry/exit timing, position
  sizing mechanics, stop-loss behavior.
- **memory/fund-notes.md** — fund-strategy reflections, objective alignment,
  risk framework decisions.

## Score 1 — Systemically wrong
Most or all lessons are in the wrong file. Trading mechanics in market-lessons,
regime observations in fund-notes, etc.

> Example: "NVDA trim after CPI hot print" written to fund-notes instead of
> trading-patterns or market-lessons. Score: 1.

## Score 2 — Many misrouted
More than half the lessons are in the wrong file. Some correct placements
appear incidentally.

## Score 3 — Partially correct
About half routed correctly. Common mistake: mixing market observations
(macro, regime) with trading patterns (sizing, timing).

## Score 4 — Mostly correct
At most one lesson is in the wrong file. The misrouting is a borderline case
(e.g., a lesson that could plausibly belong in either market-lessons or
trading-patterns).

## Score 5 — Perfectly routed
Every lesson is in the correct file. Regime/sector/macro → market-lessons,
setup/timing/sizing → trading-patterns, strategy/objective reflections →
fund-notes. No borderline misplacements.

> Example: "Transition regime (score 2.1) favors cyclicals over defensives"
> → market-lessons. "Half-size new entries when regime score is 1.5-2.5"
> → trading-patterns. "Risk-budget rebalancing after macro surprises is
> preferred to thesis-driven exits" → fund-notes. Score: 5.
